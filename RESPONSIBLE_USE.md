# Responsible use

CLOAK is provided for experimentation, education, and personal privacy awareness. Its purpose is to help a person understand how details in their own posts could combine to expose information they did not intend to share.

## Permitted context

Use fictional examples, your own text, or material supplied with informed consent for this specific analysis. Explain to anyone providing text that it will be processed by the configured external model service and that local files will retain analysis outputs. Public visibility alone is not consent.

## Prohibited uses

Do not use CLOAK to:

- Stalk, harass, doxx, threaten, or track a person.
- Identify or locate an anonymous or pseudonymous social media user.
- Link identities across accounts or platforms without informed consent.
- Collect or analyze other people's posting histories for unauthorized profiling or surveillance.
- Perform mass deanonymization or automated population-level identity analysis.
- Use inferred attributes to make consequential decisions about another person.

This distribution supplies a text-input experiment. It does not include account scraping, social media account lookup, or automated publishing. Users must not extend or combine it with other systems for prohibited purposes.

## Interpret output cautiously

Candidate counts, risk labels, and phrase scores are model-generated estimates. They are not verified facts about an individual, calibrated identification probabilities, or evidence that a person has been identified. Suggested edits are not guarantees of anonymity and may alter meaning or increase disclosure. Review suggestions yourself before deciding what to share.

## Data handling

Keep credentials private. Submit only text you are authorized to send to the API provider. Protect and delete local profiles, logs, raw model responses, and other outputs as appropriate. Do not publish another person's text or inferred attributes.
